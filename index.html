How to Play

Goal: beat each blind's score target before your hands run out. Clear Small → Big → Boss blind, then the next Ante begins. Survive 8 Antes to win.

Scoring: play up to 5 cards forming a poker hand. Score = chips × mult. Only cards that form the hand score.

Shop: buy Jokers (passive effects, max 5), Booster Packs (Tarot/Planet/Joker picks), and Vouchers (permanent upgrades). Sell anything for half price.

Tarot cards enhance your deck: Bonus, Mult, Wild, Glass, Steel, Gold, Lucky, Stone… Planet cards level up a poker hand permanently (+chips / +mult).

Boss Blinds have nasty effects: debuffed suits, no discards, 1-hand-only…

Each hand is shown with example cards — the repeated pattern is what scores. A ★ card is a copy made with a Tarot card; the last three hands need more copies of a card than a normal deck holds.
