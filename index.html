How to Play

Goal: beat each blind's score target before your hands run out. Clear Small → Big → Boss blind, then the next Ante begins — harder targets. Survive all 8 Antes to win.

Scoring: play up to 5 cards forming a poker hand. Score = chips × mult. Scoring cards add chips (number = value, face = 10, ace = 11). Only cards that form the hand score — kickers don't.

Money: blind reward + $1 per unused hand + $1 interest per $5 banked. Spend it in the Shop on Jokers (max 5) — passive effects that boost your score.
